OPEN MODEL

How everything is scored

The same five inputs, the same weights, every week — weighted by what has historically mattered in retroactive distributions.

Confirmation30%
Value size25%
Cost20%
Time demand15%
Competition10%
S ≥ 8.0A ≥ 6.5B ≥ 5.0C < 5.0

Cost 10 = genuinely $0 (testnet, faucets, quests). Capital-at-risk plays are capped low and always flagged — that's trading, not farming, and we don't pump it.

The model EV = 0.30·confirm + 0.25·value + 0.20·cost + 0.15·time + 0.10·competition Efficiency = EV ÷ max(hrs/week, 0.5) Tier: S ≥ 8.0 · A ≥ 6.5 · B ≥ 5.0 · C < 5.0

Efficiency is the sort key — it answers the only question that matters: what do I get per hour of my life?
